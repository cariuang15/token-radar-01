How the model works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120% and weighted 0.6. Maturity is 0.6 + up to 0.4 for up to three years listed with no token; a protocol with no listing date is scored as one year old. Chains adds 4% per chain past the first, up to 20%.

Category multiples

Implied dollars per dollar of TVL. A derivatives venue keeps a share of the flow it routes; a bridge moves value it does not keep, so the market has paid less per dollar of TVL for one. A category not named here takes 0.25.

What the board is not

  • Not a price. Implied value is a model of what a launch could be worth, built only from this snapshot. It is not a prediction.
  • No token ≠ no token ever. The snapshot records no token for these protocols; it does not say one is coming, or when.
  • Wrapped products are out. A staked, wrapped or bridged product of a token that already trades is not a token that does not exist yet, whatever its TVL.
  • Farm at your own risk. Never pay to claim an airdrop. Claim only from the protocol's own published link, and only with funds you can lose.
